Thematic Research Clusters

Filter by academic domain or search specific methodologies, algorithms, and applications across our 16 research divisions.

Computational Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Deep learning architectures, reinforcement learning, predictive mathematical models, explainable AI, and neural network optimization.

Decision Sciences

Business Intelligence

Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA/MCDM), enterprise data mining, market forecasting, operations research, and regression modeling.

Visual Computing

Computer Vision

Semantic segmentation, 3D point-cloud reconstruction, visual SLAM, edge vision computing, and high-resolution spatial feature extraction.

Linguistics & AI

Natural Language Processing

Large Language Models (LLMs), low-resource vernacular translations, knowledge graph mining, computational semantics, and text synthesis.

Mechatronics

Robotics

Kinematics & trajectory planning, Human-Robot Interaction (HRI), autonomous manipulation, robotic compliance, and bio-inspired mechanisms.

Aerial Systems

Drone Technology

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), swarm coordination algorithms, custom flight controllers, and autonomous aerial geospatial mapping.

Digital Health

Healthcare Science & Technology

Smart point-of-care diagnostics, tele-health frameworks, clinical decision support architectures, and continuous bio-metric wearables.

Bio-Computation

Biomedical Signal & Image Processing

ECG/EEG/EMG artifact removal, MRI/CT volumetric semantic segmentation, wavelet decomposition, and automated pathological classification.

Electromagnetics

Antenna & Microwave Engineering

Microstrip patch antennas, mmWave/THz RF structures, metamaterial surfaces, phased array beamforming, and computational electromagnetics.

Telecommunications

Wireless Technology

5G/6G experimental testbeds, massive MIMO architectures, Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS), and vehicular ad-hoc networks.

Fluid & Thermal Physics

Fluid Dynamics

Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), turbulent flow modeling, aerodynamic drag reduction, microfluidics, and multiphase transport phenomena.

Civil Infrastructure

Structural & Geotechnical Engineering

Earthquake engineering, structural health monitoring, soil-structure interaction, computational geomechanics, and sustainable resilient materials.

Sustainability

Renewable & Clean Energy

Next-generation photovoltaics, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), microgrid load optimization, and green hydrogen synthesis.

Human Performance

Sports Science

Athletic kinematics, physiological conditioning metrics, real-time load management, and data-driven injury prevention modeling.

Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

Musculoskeletal therapeutics, computerized gait analytics, sensor-assisted ergonomic assessment, and neuro-rehabilitation monitoring.

Indian Knowledge Systems

Indic Knowledge Systems (IKS)

Computational Sanskrit analysis, foundational mathematics, traditional metallurgical technologies, and ancient scientific traditions.
